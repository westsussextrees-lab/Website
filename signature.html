Adding it to Gmail

  1. Press Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the cog (top right), then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Give it a name such as "Max".
  4. Click inside the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V on a Mac).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for both new emails and replies.
  6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes.

The Gmail phone app only supports a plain-text signature, so on your phone use: Max, Tree Surgeon, West Sussex Tree Specialists, 07719 724463.
